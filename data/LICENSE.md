# Data licence

The measurements AgentRank produces are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
That covers the files in this directory and `scripts/tests/stats-vectors.json`: the cohort and
its registered questions and answers, the recorded fields of every trial, the Lighthouse rows,
extracts and manifests, the scanner panel, the reconstruction, and the statistics.

**Not covered.** Content that belongs to the measured websites and was captured incidentally is
not AgentRank's to license and remains its owners'. That includes the screenshots, page text and
other page content inside the raw Lighthouse reports under `data/lhr/` and inside the agent
transcripts. It is kept so that each measurement can be checked, not republished.
